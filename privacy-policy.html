Privacy Policy for Neon Dash: Jump Runner

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Neon Dash: Jump Runner ("we", "our", or "the Game") handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

Neon Dash: Jump Runner is designed to be played offline and respects your privacy. We do not collect or store personal identification information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location.

2. Local Game Data

The game stores gameplay statistics locally on your device (such as your High Score, total collected gems, and audio settings). This data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded to any external server.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game integrates third-party advertising services provided by Google AdMob to display rewarded video advertisements. Google AdMob may use device identifiers and cookies to serve relevant ads according to Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more about Google's advertising privacy practices at Google Advertising & Privacy.

4. Children's Privacy

Our game is designed for all audiences and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer team.
